# Phase status and verification

**As of 30 September 2026.** This records implemented behavior, not intended architecture.

P0 configuration evidence contract v1 has CLI and local API/dashboard input:
sanitized snapshots can be matched by capture hash, peer pair, one unambiguous
session and a 24-hour collection window. Matched values are separately labeled
`CONFIGURED`; invalid or partial evidence remains unknown. See
[configuration evidence v1](CONFIGURATION_EVIDENCE_V1.md). Four configured
control rules, a versioned coverage-gated risk score and evidence-linked threat
rows now appear in JSON, dashboard and text/HTML/PDF reports. The score is a
limited project-policy assessment of configured intent, not installed SA
attestation. The dashboard now uses dedicated local pages for assessment,
threats, sessions, flows, inference, evidence, packets and reports. The
submission video and full strong/weak live demo remain open.

The [SIH-aligned implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION_PHASES.md) and
[project improvement plan](PROJECT_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md) now include an
authorized-configuration evidence path, broader assessment, a coverage-gated
risk score, threat matrix, paired remediation verification and final submission
package. Paired remediation and the final submission package are planned
requirements. The phase states below retain their
documented prototype scope until the new gates pass.

Against the supplied **full** problem statement, the local prototype covers
offline parsing, the reviewed testbed cases, bounded live input, the synthetic
AI pilot, limited security rules, separate analyst pages and reports. It does
not yet support real-application traffic identity, independently validated AI
confidence, comprehensive authentication/SA/cipher/metadata assessment,
installed-state proof for hidden settings, representative live-load claims or
the demonstration video. The [problem-statement gap map](PROJECT_IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md#problem-statement-coverage-and-next-proof)
lists each capability and the next evidence required. The bounded risk score
must not be presented as a comprehensive deployment certification.
